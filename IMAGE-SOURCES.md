# TCAST Cargo image sources

The website uses the supplied TCAST logo plus the royalty-free photographs listed below. All photographs were downloaded on 2 August 2026 and are stored locally as optimized WebP files under `public/images/`. They are used as illustrative stock photography and are not presented as photographs of TCAST Cargo operations.

Unless otherwise noted, the photographs are available for free use under the [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license). Source pages remain the authoritative record for the photographer and license status.

## Supplied brand assets

- `public/brand/tcast-logo.png` is an optimized, background-cleaned copy of the logo supplied by TCAST Cargo for this project. The artwork was not redrawn or replaced.
- `public/brand/tcast-icon.png`, `public/brand/apple-touch-icon.png` and `public/brand/favicon.png` are crops derived from that supplied logo for application and browser icons.
- `public/og.png` is a project-specific social preview generated from the supplied logo and the approved TCAST blue/orange visual system. It is used only for link previews.
